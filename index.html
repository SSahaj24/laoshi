Quick Reference

Type pinyin to see if you've learned the character for it.

Separate syllables with spaces, and put the tone number (1–4) right after the letter that carries the mark — not at the end of the syllable.

三十五 sa1n shi2 wu3 · 二 e4r · 零 li2ng · 綠 lv4 (v for ü)

Typed tone marks work too — sān shí wǔ.
